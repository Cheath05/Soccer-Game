# Step 2.3b part 2: pass pace (30 Sep 2026)

**The question.** Engine ground passes were slow. With the old pace (4 m/s² rolling friction, arriving at 5 m/s), the ball needs about 1.26 s for 9.5 m and 2.15 s for 20 m. Metrica's open data (`20260930-pass-pace-metrica.md`) gives 0.99 s and 1.60 s.

**Two pairs of values reproduce Metrica's travel times exactly in the ball physics:**
- **p33:** friction 3.3 m/s², arriving at 6.0 + 0.20 m/s per metre;
- **p40:** friction 4.0 m/s² (unchanged), arriving at 6.0 + 0.17 m/s per metre.

They differ in what else they move. p33 lowers the friction of every rolling ball: loose balls, deflections, clearances. p40 changes only how hard passes are struck.

**Measured.** f020e2 settings, seed 21, 200 matches each, against 2.3b part 1. The reports are local, under `reports/engine/step2.3/ref-2.3b2/` (p33) and `ref-2.3b2-p40/`. ENG1 was also run with the committed length error, in `ref-2.3b2-e0/`.

| | ENG4 b1 | ENG4 p33 | ENG4 p40 | ENG1 b1 | ENG1 p33 | ENG1 p40 | Target (per match) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Interceptions | 21.4 | 15.9 | 15.7 | 20.1 | 15.4 | 15.7 | 14–26 |
| High regains | 47.4 | 35.0 | 41.8 | 35.8 | 26.1 | 31.2 | 10–17 |
| Throw-ins | 25.1 | 45.0 | 37.8 | 41.4 | 59.9 | 52.2 | PL 32–42; EFL 32–44 |
| Ball in play (min) | 70.2 | 63.0 | 65.3 | 61.6 | 56.2 | 58.1 | PL 54–60; EFL 52–60 |

| Loose-ball failures | 112 | 71 | 93 | 78 | 51 | 64 | |
| Completion time, medium (s) | 2.21 | 2.33 | 2.20 | 2.23 | 2.30 | 2.18 | Metrica 1.60 |

**Decision: p40.**
- Both match the evidence about pass kinematics, and that evidence says nothing about other rolling balls.
- p40 changes only the mechanism the evidence covers; the other rolling balls keep the physics they were tuned with.
- p33's extra drop in high regains comes with even more throw-ins and less ball in play, through a change with no evidence behind it.

**Conflicts, recorded and not tuned away:**
1. **Measured completion times don't follow the ball physics.**
   - Medium passes still take about 2.2 s to be completed, against Metrica's 1.6 s, with any pace. A diagnostic of four matches showed the intended receivers' median at 1.9 s, with a long tail (90th percentile 4.5 s).
   - So the time is set by how receivers reach the ball (read delay, chasing misdirected or under-hit balls, the f020e2 length error), not by ball speed.
   - Belongs to 2.3c (honest estimates: realistic options) and 2.3e (execution and reading terms).
2. **ENG1's throw-ins are too many with realistic pace:** 52, or 0.90 a minute of ball in play against 0.56–0.74.
   - A firm pass that misses its man now rolls on about 11 m instead of 3 before stopping, and more of them reach the touchline. The f020e2 length error is part of it: with the committed length error and p33, ENG1's throw-ins fall from 59.9 to 54.6.
   - The cause is how often passes miss their man. That's for 2.3e, with the read delay, the receiver's control and execution error tuned through ratings, not by slowing passes down again.
